About

Budapest Tunderi

“Tunderi” means “nights” in Kazakh: Budapest nights. The name also nods to Almatynyn Tunderi-ai, the well-known Kazakh song that carries the feeling of a city after dark.

The project started from Almaty nightlife energy and a small circle of Central Asian students in Budapest who wanted to meet while far from home. It has grown into a warmer gathering point for more people.

Comfort is not only a place or a price. It is being with the right people, where the night feels familiar, welcoming, and alive.
